# Adidas US Retail Sales & Profitability Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive executive dashboard analyzing Adidas US retail sales performance, regional profitability, and product category trends to uncover revenue drivers and margin opportunities.

## 📊 Project Overview
- **Goal:** Track total sales, operating profit, units sold, and profit margins across US regions, retailers, and sales methods.
- **Tools Used:** Microsoft Power BI, Power Query (ETL & Data Cleaning), DAX (Calculated Measures & Time Intelligence).

## 🔍 Key Insights & Takeaways
- **Top Performing Regions:** [e.g., West & Northeast] generated the highest share of total revenue, led by strong retail demand in [e.g., New York & California].
- **Product Category Performance:** [e.g., Men's Street Footwear] ranked #1 in overall sales volume and revenue contribution.
- **Sales Channel Mix:** [e.g., In-Store vs. Online / Outlet] revealed clear differences in unit volume versus operating profit margin.
- **Seasonality Trends:** Sales peaked during [e.g., Q3 / mid-year], highlighting seasonal demand spikes across key product lines.

## 🛠️ Data Modeling & DAX Highlights
- Cleaned and standardized raw retail transaction records in **Power Query** (handling data types, formatting, and date dimensions).
- Built custom **DAX measures** for Total Sales, Total Operating Profit, Average Profit Margin %, Total Units Sold, and Month-over-Month (MoM) growth.
- Designed interactive slicers for **Region, Retailer, Product Category, Sales Method, and Year/Month** for dynamic drill-down analysis.
